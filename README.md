# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis 🛒📊

## 📖 Project Overview
A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty. The management team noticed changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels, and wants to uncover which factors—such as discounts, reviews, seasons, or payment preferences—drive consumer decisions and repeat purchases. 

This project aims to answer a key business question: *"How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?"*

## 📂 Dataset Summary
The project analyzes a consumer behavior dataset representing various retail transactions.
*   **Total Rows:** 3,900
*   **Total Columns:** 18
*   **Key Features:** Contains customer demographics (Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status), purchase details (Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color), and shopping behavior (Discount Applied, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type).
*   **Data Quality:** 37 missing values were identified in the Review Rating column and handled during the cleaning phase.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
*   **Python (Pandas, SQLAlchemy, psycopg2):** Used for data loading, exploratory data analysis (EDA), handling missing values, feature engineering, and database integration.
*   **SQL (PostgreSQL):** Used to organize data into a structured format, simulate business transactions, and run queries extracting insights on customer segments and loyalty.
*   **Power BI:** Used to build an interactive dashboard highlighting key patterns, trends, and revenue metrics.
*   **Gamma:** Used to create the final presentation and report visually communicating insights and actionable recommendations to stakeholders.

## 🚀 Project Steps

### 1. Data Preparation & EDA (Python)
*   **Data Loading & Exploration:** Imported `customer_shopping_behavior.csv` using Pandas and explored initial structure and summary statistics.
*   **Missing Data Handling:** Imputed the 37 missing values in the `Review Rating` column by using the median rating of each respective product category.
*   **Data Cleaning:** Renamed columns to snake_case for better readability and dropped the `promo_code_used` column after confirming it was redundant with `discount_applied`.
*   **Feature Engineering:** Created an `age_group` column by binning customer ages and a `purchase_frequency_days` column mapped from the purchase frequency data.
*   **Database Integration:** Connected the Python script to a PostgreSQL database (`customer_behavior`) and loaded the cleaned DataFrame into a SQL table for advanced querying.

### 2. Data Analysis (SQL)
Structured analysis was performed in PostgreSQL to answer 10 key business questions, including:
*   Comparing total revenue generated by male versus female customers.
*   Identifying high-spending customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.
*   Finding the top 5 products with the highest average review ratings.
*   Comparing average purchase amounts between standard and express shipping types.
*   Comparing average spend and total revenue across subscription statuses.
*   Segmenting customers into "New", "Returning", and "Loyal" segments based on their total number of previous purchases.
*   Calculating the total revenue contribution of each age group.

### 3. Visualization & Dashboard (Power BI)
An interactive Power BI dashboard was built to enable stakeholders to make data-driven decisions. 
*   **KPIs:** Highlighted 3.9K total customers, a $59.76 average purchase amount, and a 3.75 average review rating.
*   **Visuals:** Included charts for "% of Customers by Subscription Status", "Revenue by Category", "Sales by Category", "Revenue by Age Group", and "Sales by Age Group".

## 💡 Key Results & Insights
*   **Demographic Revenue:** Male customers generated significantly more revenue ($157,890) compared to female customers ($75,191).
*   **Top Products:** The highest-rated products are Gloves (3.86), Sandals (3.84), and Boots (3.82).
*   **Subscription Impact:** Non-subscribers generated higher total revenue ($170,436) than subscribers ($62,645), though the average spend per transaction was nearly identical.
*   **Customer Loyalty:** The vast majority of the customer base falls into the "Loyal" segment (3,116 customers), compared to "Returning" (701) and "New" (83).
*   **Revenue by Age:** The "Young Adult" age group contributed the highest total revenue ($62,143), followed closely by "Middle-aged" ($59,197).

## 🎯 Business Recommendations
Based on the data analysis, the following actionable recommendations were presented to the stakeholders:
1.  **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits for subscribers to increase adoption.
2.  **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers to transition more users into the highly profitable "Loyal" segment.
3.  **Review Discount Policy:** Balance the sales boosts gained from discounts with margin control to optimize profitability.
4.  **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in future marketing campaigns.
5.  **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and express-shipping users.
